## Decision

Arthur's proposal was accepted: a `step_viewed` event for each of the 3 quiz steps, so a visit produces 9 events instead of 6. Reasons recorded in A-1: answer events alone do not show a return that left the answer unchanged or the time spent on a step; a changed answer is a product signal; Q-13 defines "started the quiz" as step 1 shown. The design is sized for 18M events a month against the spec's 12M, and `nfr.md` shows both.
