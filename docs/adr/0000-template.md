# NNNN. Title in the form of a short decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN (link the new file)
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Relates to: R-/Q-/A- ids from `docs/requirements/`

## Context

What forces the decision: the requirement, constraint or problem, with a link to the spec section or `docs/requirements/` entry.

## Constraints

Limits that shaped the choice and would change it if they changed: budget, platform limits, deadlines, team experience, legal.

## Options considered

1. Option A: how it works, what it costs, where it breaks.
2. Option B: same.

## Decision

The option chosen and the main reason.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now have to watch.

## Verification

How we will know the decision holds: a test, a load check, a metric, a limit to monitor.

## References

Documentation, limits pages, research notes and measurements the options above rely on.

## Outcome

Filled in after the implementing stage passed its verification. Date, what held, what did not, and whether a new ADR is needed.
